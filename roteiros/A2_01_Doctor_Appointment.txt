TITLE: Doctor Appointment
LEVEL: Elementary (A2)

AUDIO SCRIPT

RECEPTIONIST: Good morning. How can I help you?
DAVID: I would like an appointment with Dr Patel.
RECEPTIONIST: Is it urgent?
DAVID: No, but I have had a sore throat for three days.
RECEPTIONIST: Dr Patel is free tomorrow at eleven.
DAVID: Can I come after work instead?
RECEPTIONIST: She has an appointment at four thirty.
DAVID: That is perfect.
RECEPTIONIST: Please bring your health card.
DAVID: I will. Thank you.
