TITLE: At the Supermarket
LEVEL: Beginner (A1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

NORA: Hi, Ben. What do we need?
BEN: We need bread and eggs.
NORA: Do we need milk?
BEN: Yes, please. Two bottles.
NORA: Okay. The bread is here.
BEN: Great. The eggs are next to the cheese.
NORA: I can see them.
BEN: Can we get apples too?
NORA: Yes. Let's get six apples.
BEN: Perfect. That is all.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] They need two bottles of milk.
[F] The eggs are next to the bread.
[T] They decide to buy six apples.
