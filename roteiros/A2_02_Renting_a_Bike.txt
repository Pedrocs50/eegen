TITLE: Renting a Bike
LEVEL: Elementary (A2)

AUDIO SCRIPT

CLERK: Welcome to Riverside Bikes. How long do you need the bike?
KIM: For one day, please.
CLERK: Would you like a helmet as well?
KIM: Yes, and a map of the river path.
CLERK: Certainly. The blue route is easy and takes about an hour.
KIM: That sounds nice. How much is the rental?
CLERK: Fifteen euros, including the helmet.
KIM: Can I pay by card?
CLERK: Of course. Please return the bike before six o'clock.
KIM: I will. Thanks for your help.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] Kim rents a bike for one day.
[F] The blue route is difficult.
[T] The helmet is included in the price.
