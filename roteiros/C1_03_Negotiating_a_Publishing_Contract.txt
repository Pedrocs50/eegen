TITLE: Negotiating a Publishing Contract
LEVEL: Advanced (C1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

EDITOR: We are enthusiastic about the manuscript, but the first print run needs to be modest. The market for this subject is specialised.
AUTHOR: I understand the risk, although a small print run could make the book harder to find when reviews appear.
EDITOR: That is why we propose a simultaneous digital release. If demand exceeds expectations, we can reprint quickly without asking readers to wait.
AUTHOR: I would also like the contract to protect my right to use short extracts in my teaching.
EDITOR: That is reasonable, provided the extracts are clearly credited and do not replace the book itself.
AUTHOR: One final point: the proposed deadline is ambitious because I am still checking several sources.
EDITOR: We would rather receive a carefully verified manuscript in September than a rushed one in July. Let us amend the schedule.
AUTHOR: In that case, I am happy to move forward.

TRUE OR FALSE

[F] The editor wants a very large first print run.
[T] The contract will allow credited extracts for teaching.
[F] The editor prefers a rushed July manuscript.

MULTIPLE CHOICE

? Why does the editor propose a modest print run?
* The subject has a specialised market
- The author requested it
- Paper is unavailable

? What would happen if demand increases?
* The book could be reprinted quickly
- The digital edition would end
- The contract would be cancelled

? Why is the author concerned about the deadline?
* Sources still need checking
- The book is already late
- The editor is unavailable
