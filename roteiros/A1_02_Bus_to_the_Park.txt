TITLE: Bus to the Park
LEVEL: Beginner (A1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

DRIVER: Hello. Where are you going?
ELLA: To the park, please.
DRIVER: The city park?
ELLA: Yes, the city park.
DRIVER: Take bus number twelve.
ELLA: Where does it stop?
DRIVER: It stops in front of the park.
ELLA: How much is the ticket?
DRIVER: Two pounds.
ELLA: Here you are.
DRIVER: Thank you. The bus leaves at ten.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] Ella wants to go to the city park.
[F] She needs bus number twenty.
[T] The ticket costs two pounds.
