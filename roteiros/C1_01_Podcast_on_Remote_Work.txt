TITLE: Podcast on Remote Work
LEVEL: Advanced (C1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

HOST: Your report argues that remote work should remain flexible rather than mandatory. Why?
DR LEE: Because the evidence is mixed. Many employees value autonomy and avoid commuting, yet some teams lose the informal contact that helps newer staff learn.
HOST: So a fully remote policy is not automatically more productive?
DR LEE: Exactly. Productivity depends less on location than on clear objectives, appropriate tools, and managers who trust people without monitoring every minor activity.
HOST: What is the most common mistake organisations make?
DR LEE: They copy another company's policy without asking what their own employees actually need. A small design team and a large call centre may require entirely different arrangements.
HOST: What would you recommend to a company starting from scratch?
DR LEE: Run a short trial, collect both performance data and staff feedback, then adjust the policy rather than treating the first version as permanent.
