TITLE: Weekend Trip Plans
LEVEL: Pre-Intermediate (B1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

LEO: Have you decided where to go this weekend?
PRIYA: I was thinking of visiting Brighton, but the weather forecast looks rainy.
LEO: Why not go to Oxford instead? There are museums if it rains.
PRIYA: That is a good idea. Shall we take the early train on Saturday?
LEO: Yes. It leaves at eight twenty, so we should meet at the station at eight.
PRIYA: Fine. I will book the tickets tonight.
LEO: Great. I can find a small hotel near the centre.
PRIYA: Do not spend too much. I am saving for my summer holiday.
LEO: I will look for something reasonable.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] Priya first considered visiting Brighton.
[F] They choose Oxford because the forecast is sunny.
[T] Leo will look for a reasonably priced hotel.
