TITLE: A Lost Jacket
LEVEL: Elementary (A2)

AUDIO SCRIPT

MAYA: Excuse me, I think I left my jacket here yesterday.
MANAGER: Can you describe it?
MAYA: It is black, with a small red button near the pocket.
MANAGER: Was there anything inside?
MAYA: Yes, my train card and a pair of gloves.
MANAGER: Let me check our lost-property box.
MAYA: Thank you.
MANAGER: Is this it?
MAYA: Yes, that is my jacket. I am so relieved.
MANAGER: Please write your name and phone number here.
MAYA: No problem.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] Maya lost her jacket yesterday.
[F] The jacket is blue.
[T] Her train card was inside the jacket.
