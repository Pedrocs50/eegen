TITLE: Office Renovation Meeting
LEVEL: Intermediate (B2)

AUDIO SCRIPT

MARTIN: Before we approve the renovation plan, we need to discuss the disruption to staff.
HELEN: The builders can work on the third floor first, while that team uses the meeting rooms downstairs.
MARTIN: How long would that arrangement last?
HELEN: Approximately three weeks. After that, the team could return upstairs and the builders would move to the second floor.
MARTIN: What about the customer-service team? They cannot work in a noisy room.
HELEN: I have spoken to the landlord. We can use two empty offices in the next building at no additional cost.
MARTIN: That solves the main problem. However, we should tell clients about possible delays.
HELEN: Agreed. I will draft an email for you to review this afternoon.
