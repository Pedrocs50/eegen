TITLE: After Work
LEVEL: Beginner (A1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

JAMES: Hi, Ana. Are you busy after work?

JAMES: No problem. Do you like pizza?

JAMES: Great. Let's meet at Pizza House at seven.
ANA: Seven is good. See you there.
JAMES: See you later.
