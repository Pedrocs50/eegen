TITLE: The Train Service Debate
LEVEL: Advanced (C1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

MODERATOR: Critics say the proposed night train is too expensive for the number of passengers it will carry. How do you respond?
COUNCILLOR: The estimate looks only at ticket sales. It ignores reduced road congestion, safer journeys for late-shift workers, and the fact that reliable transport can support local businesses.
MODERATOR: But those benefits are difficult to measure, are they not?
COUNCILLOR: They are not as immediate as fare revenue, certainly, but that does not make them imaginary. We can compare accident rates and traffic levels before and after the service begins.
MODERATOR: Would you cancel the plan if demand remained low after a year?
COUNCILLOR: I would revise the timetable first. A service that runs at inconvenient times may appear unpopular even when the underlying need is real.
MODERATOR: So you favour evaluation, but not a single measure of success.
COUNCILLOR: Precisely. Public transport should be assessed through its wider social effect as well as its balance sheet.
