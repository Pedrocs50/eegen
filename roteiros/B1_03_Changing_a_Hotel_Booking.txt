TITLE: Changing a Hotel Booking
LEVEL: Pre-Intermediate (B1)

AUDIO SCRIPT

AGENT: Good afternoon, Harbour Hotel. How may I assist you?
OLIVER: I have a booking for Friday, but my flight has been cancelled.
AGENT: I am sorry to hear that. Would you like to move the booking to Saturday?
OLIVER: Yes, if possible. I will arrive late, probably around ten in the evening.
AGENT: That is fine. Your room will be available until midnight.
OLIVER: Is there an extra charge for changing the date?
AGENT: No, because you called more than twenty-four hours before arrival.
OLIVER: Excellent. Could you send me the new confirmation by email?
AGENT: Certainly. I will send it in the next few minutes.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] Oliver’s flight was cancelled.
[F] He will arrive before six in the evening.
[F] The hotel charges him to change the date.
