TITLE: Community Garden Proposal
LEVEL: Intermediate (B2)

AUDIO SCRIPT

CHAIR: Your proposal mentions a community garden behind the library. What would residents gain from it?
AMINA: Besides growing vegetables, the garden would offer weekly workshops for children and older residents.
CHAIR: Some neighbours are concerned about the cost of maintaining it.
AMINA: We have approached three local businesses. They are prepared to donate tools and seeds for the first year.
CHAIR: And after that?
AMINA: The garden could sell a small amount of produce to a nearby cafe. The income would cover basic supplies, although volunteers would still be essential.
CHAIR: Have you considered access for people with limited mobility?
AMINA: Yes. The paths would be wide enough for wheelchairs, and several planting beds would be raised.
CHAIR: Thank you. The committee will consider the proposal next week.

TRUE OR FALSE

[T] The garden would offer workshops.
[F] The businesses will pay all costs forever.
[T] The plan includes wheelchair access.
